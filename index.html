Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Innovation

Founded in 2005 by renowned Chef Alexandre Dubois, Éclat has consistently pushed the boundaries of contemporary cuisine while honoring classic techniques. Our Michelin-starred kitchen team creates unforgettable dining experiences that engage all the senses.

Each season, we curate a new tasting menu that reflects the finest local and international ingredients, paired with an exceptional wine selection from our sommelier.
